Please read before you start tracking your money

Effective as of your first use. By checking the box below and continuing, you agree to the following terms governing your use of FINUITY ("the App"). Please read this in full.

1. What FINUITY Is

FINUITY is a personal, self-managed finance tracker for recording income, expenses, wallet balances (e.g. GCash, Maya, Cash on Hand), loans, and savings goals that you enter yourself. It is a personal/academic project, currently under active development.

2. What FINUITY Is Not

FINUITY is not a bank, e-wallet, remittance provider, lending company, payment processor, or financial institution. It does not move, hold, transmit, or have any connection to your actual money, bank accounts, or e-wallet accounts. All wallet balances and loan records are figures you type in for your own reference only. Nothing in the App constitutes financial, investment, tax, or legal advice, and any devotional or scripture content included is for personal encouragement only, not professional or pastoral counsel.

3. Your Data & Your Account

A copy of your information is stored locally in your browser's storage (localStorage) on the device you're using, and also synced to a cloud database (Firebase/Firestore) tied to the account you signed in with (Google, email, or an anonymous guest account), so your data can follow you across devices. The developer does not manually access, view, or use your individual data. If you sign in as a Guest, your account has no email attached, so if you lose access to this device or clear its storage, that data cannot be recovered or linked back to you.

4. You Are Responsible for Backups

Because your data lives only in this browser, it can be permanently lost if you clear your browsing data/cache, use private/incognito mode, switch browsers or devices, uninstall or reset the browser, or if local storage becomes corrupted. FINUITY provides a manual Backup/Export feature (Settings). You are solely responsible for creating and safekeeping your own backups; the developer is not responsible for any data loss.

5. PIN Lock Is a Convenience Feature Only

The 4-digit PIN restricts casual access to the App on your device. It is not encryption and is not equivalent to bank-grade security. Anyone with sufficient technical access to your device or browser storage may be able to view or alter the underlying data. Do not treat the PIN as a guarantee of security.

6. No Warranty

FINUITY is provided "as is" and "as available," without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including accuracy, reliability, or fitness for a particular purpose. As an evolving student/personal project, it may contain bugs, incomplete features, or occasional errors in calculations, and features or branding may change over time.

7. Limitation of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the developer shall not be liable for any financial decisions you make based on information in the App, nor for any direct, indirect, incidental, or consequential loss — including loss of data or loss arising from reliance on figures recorded in the App.

8. Eligibility

FINUITY is intended for your own personal, individual use. If you are a minor, please use the App only with the awareness and consent of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to These Terms

These terms may be updated as the App develops. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

10. Ending Your Use

You may stop using the App at any time and permanently delete all locally stored data via Settings → Clear All Data. This action cannot be undone.
